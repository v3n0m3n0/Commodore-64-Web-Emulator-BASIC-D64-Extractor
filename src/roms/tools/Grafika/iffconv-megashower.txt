I have uploaded two utilities, namely: the Iff-converter v1.0 and the
latest version of the Megashower (v1.02). Both utilities are done by
Scroll/Megastyle (me), and they are now ntsc-fixed too.

The purpose of the iff-converter speaks for itself, it converts an iff or lbm
picture into one of many existing c64 picture formats (art studio and afli).
The Megashower is a browser/shower program for different kinds of picture
and music files.

Some time ago I discovered an early version of the Iff-converter (v0.99)
on this ftp, it was not supposed to be spread, but anyway here's the new
version.
