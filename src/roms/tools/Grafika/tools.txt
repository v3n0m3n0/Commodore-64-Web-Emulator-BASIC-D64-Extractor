Overall information:
All editors, except DPL's tools, are based on the Floating Cursor Control 
(FCC) concept. That means that there are only keyboard control.
Olav Morkrid was the first one to use this routine in his Zoomatic editors.
Later on we have added this concept to most of our editors. And we believe
it is the most human and userfriendly concept there is.
In other words: Joystick sucks!
