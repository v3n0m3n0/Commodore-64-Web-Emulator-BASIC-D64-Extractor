C64/C128 I/O performances

Numbers may change due to bugfixes and changes in IDEDOS 0.9x! For corrections
write to soci at server c64.rulez.org.

  * All systems are PAL.
  * C64/C128 means stock machine, native mode
  * 1541/1571/1581 means stock drive
  * C64J/C128J means C64/C128 with JiffyDOS 6.01
  * 1541J means 1541 with JiffyDOS 5.0
  * 1571J/1581J means 1571/1581 with JiffyDOS 6.0
  * SCPU means SuperCPU64 at 20MHz
  * ID means IDEDOS (IDE64 cartridge)
  * CHS/LBA means accessing mode of harddisk

    Time is represented in seconds for the operation with a 185 block program.
